About us

Built by students, for students.

We're a team of computer science students at Wilfrid Laurier University. We built Trackr because we were living the problem ourselves — five syllabi, a dozen deadlines, and no calm place to see it all in one spot.

Our goal is simple: take the busywork of tracking off your plate, so the energy you'd spend staying organized goes back into actually learning.

  • Designed around the real student workflow
  • Less time on admin, more time to learn
  • Calm by default — it should reduce stress, not add to it
